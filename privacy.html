Privacy Policy

This application is a personal automation tool built and used by a single individual for their own private use. It is not distributed to or used by the public.

It accesses Gmail data (read and send, for one dedicated mailbox; and read-only access to a personal inbox) solely to let the owner monitor and respond to their own email through an automated assistant they operate. No data collected through these scopes is shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party. Data stays on the owner's own private server and is never transmitted anywhere except as needed to operate the owner's own email accounts.
